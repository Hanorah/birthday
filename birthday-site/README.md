# Happy Birthday Uncle Mo — birthday drive

HOW TO PLAY
1. Drive: click & drag (or touch & drag) on the screen towards where you want to go — or W A S D / arrow keys, or the 🎮 on-screen arrows.
2. Follow the big YELLOW arrow to candles. Every candle flies to the birthday cake.
3. Every few candles a letter pops up automatically (at 6, 14, 22 and 30 candles).
   The letter shows a picture and is read aloud (music pauses while it's read).
Letters 1-4 open at 6, 14, 22 and 30 candles. At 39 candles the cake rises:
the arrow turns GREEN — drive to it, hold the button (or SPACE) to make a wish and blow out the candles.
Confetti, the 39th letter with the prayer, then "Happy 39th Birthday, Uncle Mo".
Cones and crates slow you down. Power-ups: ⚡ turbo, ◎ magnet (pulls candles), ✚ shield. You can drive through water.

PLAY
- On this PC: double-click Play-Drive.bat, then open http://localhost:8080 (uses audio/song.mp3 if present).
- Phone on the same Wi-Fi: http://<your-PC-IP>:8080
